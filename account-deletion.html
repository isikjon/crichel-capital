Account Deletion Request

App: Unstop VPN  |  Developer: CRICHEL CAPITAL LTD  |  Last Updated: 14 May 2026

This page explains how Unstop VPN users can request deletion of their app account and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

Unstop VPN accounts are created or linked through Telegram-based authentication. To request deletion of your account, use one of the methods below.

  1. Open the Telegram bot or Telegram login flow that you used to access Unstop VPN.
  2. Send the message: Delete my Unstop VPN account.
  3. Include your Telegram username or Telegram user ID if it is available to you.

You can also send the same request by email to info@crichelcapital.site. Please include the Telegram username or Telegram user ID connected with your Unstop VPN account so we can verify and process the request.

What Data Will Be Deleted

After verifying the request, we will delete or anonymise the app account data associated with your Unstop VPN account, including:

  • Telegram account identifier used for app authentication.
  • App profile and account status records.
  • Authentication/session records connected to the account.
  • Device and subscription access records linked to the account, where deletion is technically and legally permitted.

Data That May Be Retained

Some information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, tax, accounting, or app store compliance purposes. This may include:

  • Payment, invoice, or subscription transaction references where retention is legally required.
  • Security and server logs, normally retained for up to 12 months.
  • Support correspondence related to the deletion request.
  • Aggregated or anonymised analytics that no longer identify you.

Deleting your Unstop VPN account does not automatically cancel any active Google Play or App Store subscription. Subscriptions should be cancelled directly through the relevant app store account.

Processing Time

We aim to respond to account deletion requests within 10 business days. Verified deletion requests are normally completed within 30 days, unless a longer period is required by law or for legitimate security reasons.
