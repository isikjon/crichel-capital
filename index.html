About Us

A Trusted Partner in Corporate Strategy & Governance

CRICHEL CAPITAL LTD is a privately held company registered in England and Wales, operating from its registered address at Dean Farm, Witchampton, Wimborne, BH21 5BU. The company conducts activities across two principal business classifications: the management of holding company interests (SIC 64209) and the provision of management consultancy services (SIC 70229).

Our mission is to provide structured, disciplined, and forward-thinking corporate services to the organisations within our portfolio and to external clients who require independent advisory support. We believe that sound governance, clear strategic direction, and rigorous operational oversight are the foundations upon which lasting business value is built.

Drawing on deep expertise in corporate structures, regulatory compliance, and organisational development, CRICHEL CAPITAL LTD serves as both a steward of subsidiary interests and a trusted external adviser to businesses navigating complex strategic challenges. Our approach is methodical, evidence-based, and tailored to the specific circumstances of each engagement.

Our Services

What We Do

CRICHEL CAPITAL LTD offers a focused range of corporate services spanning holding company management and independent management consultancy, each designed to deliver measurable, lasting results.

Holding Company Management

We oversee and coordinate the activities of subsidiary entities within a corporate group structure, ensuring alignment with overarching strategic objectives. This includes managing equity interests, monitoring subsidiary performance, and ensuring that governance frameworks are consistently applied across all portfolio companies.

SIC 64209

Management Consultancy

Our consultancy practice provides independent, objective analysis and recommendations to organisations facing strategic, operational, or organisational challenges. We work alongside leadership teams to diagnose issues, develop actionable strategies, and support implementation — without taking on executive responsibilities.

SIC 70229

Strategic Planning & Advisory

We assist organisations in defining their long-term direction, setting measurable objectives, and building the operational capabilities required to achieve them. Our strategic advisory work encompasses market analysis, competitive positioning, scenario planning, and the development of multi-year business roadmaps.

Advisory

Portfolio & Investment Oversight

As a holding entity, we provide structured oversight of business interests across multiple sectors. This includes performance monitoring, risk assessment, capital allocation guidance, and the coordination of reporting obligations to ensure that each portfolio entity operates efficiently and in compliance with applicable standards.

Holding

Corporate Governance & Compliance

We help organisations establish and maintain robust governance structures, including board composition, policy frameworks, internal controls, and regulatory compliance programmes. Our work ensures that companies meet their legal obligations while embedding a culture of accountability and transparency throughout the organisation.

Governance

Organisational Development & Change

We support organisations through periods of structural change, growth, or transformation. Our organisational development services include restructuring advisory, leadership effectiveness programmes, process optimisation, and the design of scalable operating models suited to the evolving needs of the business.
